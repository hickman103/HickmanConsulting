Who I Am

I'm Hunter Hickman — a secondary educator and department leader in Indiana who spends the school day teaching, coaching wrestling, and the rest of it helping schools and educators navigate AI.

Classroom action research, PD delivered across Indiana, and no platform to sell — my only goal is to help educators make AI a positive tool that can help amplify results with purpose, discipline, and honesty. We know students are already using AI tools, but the worst possible outcome is that they use it quietly.

Seen at CIESC, Indiana Connected Educators (ICE), Purdue AI in P-12, the Indiana DOE AI Collaborative, and IASP conferences.
